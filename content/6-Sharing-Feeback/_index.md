After participating in the **First Cloud AI Journey** program, I had the opportunity to gain new knowledge about Cloud, AWS, and how to deploy solutions in real-world environments. Below are some comments and suggestions that may contribute to further improving the program.

### Overall Evaluation of the Program

**1. Training Content and Learning Roadmap**

The program content is structured from fundamental knowledge to practical implementation, helping participants gradually become familiar with the AWS environment. The labs and accompanying materials provide opportunities to actively explore and verify knowledge through hands-on practice.

**2. Workshops and Knowledge-Sharing Activities**

The workshops provided many practical perspectives from experienced professionals in the industry. The sharing sessions about how Cloud technologies are used in businesses helped provide a clearer understanding of the differences between theoretical knowledge and practical requirements when deploying systems.

**3. Opportunities for Hands-on AWS Practice**

One of the highlights of the program is the opportunity to directly practice with AWS services. Through building **VPC, Subnet, Route Table, NAT Gateway, EC2, IAM**, and monitoring services, I gained a better understanding of the relationships between components in a Cloud system.

**4. Developing Problem-Solving Skills**

During the hands-on practice, many configuration errors occurred, requiring me to proactively read logs, check configurations, and identify the causes before determining appropriate solutions. This helped improve my ability to analyze and solve problems rather than simply following predefined instructions.

**5. Connecting with the Technology Community**

The program provided an environment where interns could exchange knowledge, share experiences, and support one another throughout the learning process. This was a positive factor that increased motivation to explore Cloud and related technologies in greater depth.

---

### Survey Questions

- **What impressed you most about the program?**

  The combination of theoretical learning, workshops, and hands-on practice directly on AWS was the most impressive aspect of the program. This approach helped reinforce knowledge through the process of self-deployment and handling issues that arose during practice.

- **What could the program improve?**

  The program could add more practical scenario-based labs, where learners are given a general requirement and asked to analyze it, select appropriate services, design the architecture, and deploy the system before comparing their approach with the provided solution.

- **Would you recommend the program to other students?**

  Yes. The program is suitable for students who want to learn about Cloud and AWS through hands-on practice while gaining experience that is closer to a real-world working environment.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

- **Suggestion:** Add more **Mini Project**-based group assignments, focusing on requirements analysis, architecture design, deployment, and monitoring of a complete system on AWS.

- **Expectation:** Continue organizing workshops and practical experience-sharing sessions, while expanding topics related to **Cloud Security, DevOps, AI, and Automation on AWS**.
