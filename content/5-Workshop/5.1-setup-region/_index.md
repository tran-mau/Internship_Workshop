## Preparing the Environment & Setting up the Region  #



### Prerequisites:  

  1. **AWS Account** with Administrator Access or an IAM User with full permissions to operate Cognito, DynamoDB, Lambda, API Gateway, EC2, S3, CodeDeploy, and IAM.  

  2. **Web Browser** (Google Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Microsoft Edge).  

### Sign in to the Console and Switch Region  

  1. Access the AWS Management Console and sign in to your account.  

  2. In the top-right corner of the navigation bar, select the Region **Asia Pacific (Singapore) - ap-southeast-1**.
