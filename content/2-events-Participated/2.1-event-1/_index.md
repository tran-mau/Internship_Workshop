## "FCAJ Community" Event Report

### General Event Information

**Event Name**: AWS VIETNAM COMMUNITY MEETUP AI REVOLUTION & OPEN CLAW

**Event Date**: 25/07/2026

**Event Location**: 7th Floor, Grand Terra Tower, 36 Cat Linh, Dong Da, Hanoi.

### Event Objectives

- Update knowledge of the latest AI trends on AWS.

- Introduce AI Agent and Open-Source AI applications.

- Share approaches for choosing suitable AI Agent Patterns.

- Explore ways to turn AI into practical business value.

- Connect and exchange experiences with the AWS Vietnam community.

### Key Highlights

- **Current Situation and Challenges:** GenAI and coding assistance tools significantly increase code-writing speed (Inner Loop). However, the process of bringing products into production (Outer Loop) can still be limited by evaluation, testing, integration, and deployment steps.

- **Solution for the Outer Loop:** Instead of focusing only on building more powerful AI models, it is also important to automate processes through Lint rules, CI checks, Skills, and documentation within the Repository.

- **Infrastructure Trends:** Cloud infrastructure management is gradually shifting from a reactive approach to a proactive approach through AIOps, FinOps, and Proactive Security. The future trend is toward infrastructure that can automatically manage and optimize itself with the support of AI Agents.

- **Practical Applications of AI:** AI is increasingly becoming a supporting tool in various activities such as software development, system operations, data analysis, and infrastructure optimization.

### What I Learned

- **Changing the Way AI-Generated Code Is Evaluated:** Focus on design structure, how components communicate with each other, and risks related to state, error handling, and security instead of only checking individual lines of code.

- **Automation and Knowledge Storage:** Convert common rules and errors into documentation, Lint rules, or Skills so that they can be reused across different sessions.

- **Enhancing Testing:** Leverage AI to support the creation of Test Cases and testing tools for important functions, thereby improving system reliability.

- **Changing the Role of Engineers:** The role of engineers is increasingly shifting from directly operating systems to designing, controlling, and monitoring systems supported by AI.

### Application to the Learning Process

- **Organizing Documentation in the Repository:** Learn how to build documentation and project rules directly within the Repository to support both the development process and the use of AI Agents.

- **Understanding Inner Loop and Outer Loop:** Clarify how AI can support the process from code writing and testing to software integration and deployment.

- **Learning about AIOps and FinOps:** Approach the use of AI to detect anomalies, monitor systems, and optimize costs when operating Cloud environments.

- **Developing AI Skills:** Practice Prompt Engineering and Context Engineering, and learn about control mechanisms such as Guardrails and Rollback.

- **Connecting with AWS Knowledge:** Expand perspectives on Cloud Infrastructure, Monitoring, Cost Optimization, and Security through practical content shared at the event.

### Experience During the Event

- **Practical Perspective:** Approach AI from the perspectives of Infrastructure and Software Development rather than focusing only on AI models or Data Science.

- **Better Understanding of the Role of AI:** Recognize that AI should be used as a tool to support and extend the capabilities of engineers rather than completely replacing human thinking and evaluation.

- **Exposure to New Technology Trends:** Become familiar with concepts such as Agentic AI, AIOps, FinOps, and trends in building automated infrastructure.

- **Learning Direction:** Gain a practical perspective for learning Cloud, Infrastructure, Software Development, and AI, while identifying the skills that need to be further developed.

- **Key Takeaway:** *"Ship fast with AI, not by it"* emphasizes using AI as a tool to increase productivity, improve quality, and support the system development process.

### Lessons Learned

- **AI Is a Supporting Tool, Not a Replacement for Thinking:** Effective use of AI needs to be accompanied by the ability to verify, evaluate, and make decisions.

- **Focus on Quality Rather Than Just Speed:** Faster code generation does not necessarily mean faster product completion. Testing, integration, deployment, and system quality must also be emphasized.

- **Prioritize Automation of Repetitive Tasks:** Rules, common errors, and testing processes should be automated through Lint rules, CI/CD, and documentation.

- **Strengthen Cloud Infrastructure Knowledge:** Continue to study Monitoring, Security, Cost Optimization, and Automation in greater depth to build a strong foundation for Cloud system operations.

- **Proactively Learn about Agentic AI:** As AI Agents become increasingly involved in system development and operations, it is necessary to develop the ability to build appropriate Context, Prompts, and control mechanisms.

- **Develop System Design and Monitoring Thinking:** In addition to direct technical skills, focus on the ability to analyze architecture, identify risks, and control system operations.

- **Combine Theoretical Knowledge with Practical Experience:** The content shared at the event helped connect knowledge of AWS, Cloud, and Software Development with technology trends being applied in real-world environments.
